Privacy Policy — Numfold: Number Merge Puzzle

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clixio Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play Numfold: Number Merge Puzzle (the "App") on Android.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including optional ads you can choose to watch for in-game rewards (such as extra moves or boosters). To deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and app set ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • App interactions with ads (for example, ad views and clicks)
  • Diagnostic information (for example, performance data related to ads)

Your game progress (such as levels, stars, scores, boosters and settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

2. How this information is used

The information above is used by Google AdMob to:

  • Show ads in the App
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • Show personalized ads, only if you give consent where required by law

3. Consent (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy & ads. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Third-party services

We share data only with the following third party, which processes it under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies. Uninstalling the App deletes all game data stored on your device.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through Google's tools (see the links above). You can still contact us with any question or request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make significant changes, we will also mention them in the App's store listing or in the App.
